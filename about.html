Our Story

30 years of experience. Generations of customers.

Welcome to Al Baddar Barbers, a trusted local barbershop in the heart of Henfield High Street, proudly serving the local community for over 30 years.

For three decades, we’ve built our reputation on quality haircuts, attention to detail and friendly service. Generations of customers have trusted us with their hair, and we’re proud to continue providing the traditional barber experience while keeping up with modern styles and trends.

Whether you’re looking for a classic haircut, skin fade, modern fade, beard trim, hot towel shave, long haircut or a fresh new style, our goal is simple — to make sure every customer leaves looking sharp and feeling confident.

Our Promise

One trusted local barber.

We welcome men and boys of all ages and cater to all hair types and styles. From a traditional cut to the latest modern fade, we take the time to get the details right and give every customer a professional finish.

Located conveniently on Henfield High Street, Al Baddar Barbers is a friendly, relaxed and welcoming place where you can sit back, enjoy the barber experience and leave feeling fresh.

And if you don’t have an appointment, no problem — just walk in and get your fresh cut.
