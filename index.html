About Us

Three decades.
Generations of customers.

Welcome to Al Baddar Barbers, a trusted local barbershop in the heart of Henfield High Street, proudly serving the local community for over 30 years.

For three decades we’ve built our reputation on quality haircuts, attention to detail and friendly service. Generations of customers have trusted us with their hair, and we’re proud to continue the traditional barber experience while keeping up with modern styles and trends.

Whether you’re looking for a classic haircut, skin fade, modern fade, beard trim, hot towel shave, long haircut or a fresh new style, our goal is simple — to make sure every customer leaves looking sharp and feeling confident. We welcome men and boys of all ages and cater to all hair types.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?+

No. We’re a walk-in barbershop — just come in. If you’d like to request a time, message us on WhatsApp or call 01273 494283.

How much is a haircut?+

A gents haircut is £21. Under 14s and retired customers are £17, beard trims are £12 and a hot towel shave is £22.

Do you cut children’s hair?+

Yes. We welcome boys of all ages, and haircuts for under 14s are £17.

When are you open late?+

Every Thursday until 8pm. Monday to Wednesday and Friday we’re open 8.30am to 6pm, and Saturday and Sunday 8.30am to 5pm — we’re open 7 days a week.

Where are you?+

3 Aleys, High St, Henfield BN5 9HP — right on Henfield High Street in West Sussex.
