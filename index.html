About Us

Your Local Barbershop in Henfield

Welcome to Al-Baddar Barbers, your friendly local barber’s shop on Henfield High Street, serving customers from Henfield and the surrounding areas.

Whether you’re after a classic haircut, a sharp skin fade, a modern style, a beard trim or a traditional hot towel shave, we’ve got you covered. We take pride in quality cuts, attention to detail and friendly service.

We welcome men and boys of all ages and cater for all hair types. Pop into our shop on Henfield High Street for a fresh cut and a proper traditional barbering experience.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?+

No. We’re a walk-in barbershop — just come in. If you’d like to request a time, message us on WhatsApp or call 01273494283.

How much is a haircut?+

A gents haircut is from £21. Under 14s are from £17 and retired customers (over 67) are £17, beard trims are from £12 and a hot towel shave is from £22.

Do you cut children’s hair?+

Yes. We welcome boys of all ages, and haircuts for under 14s are from £17.

When are you open late?+

Every Thursday until 8pm. Monday to Wednesday and Friday we’re open 8.30am to 6pm, and Saturday 8.30am to 5pm. Closed on Sundays.

Where are you?+

3 Aleys, High St, Henfield BN5 9HP — right on Henfield High Street in West Sussex.
