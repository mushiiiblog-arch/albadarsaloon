Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Al-Baddar Barbers uses your information. Last updated 21 November 2026.

1. Who we are

This website is run by Al-Baddar Barbers, 3 Aleys, High St, Henfield, West Sussex BN5 9HP, United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website and in our shop. Questions about privacy: call 01273494283 or visit us in the shop.

2. What we collect and why

  • Appointment requests. When you message us on WhatsApp, call us, or use the booking form (which opens WhatsApp with your message), we receive your name, phone number and the details you send — such as the service, day and time you’d like. We use this only to reply and arrange your visit. The booking form itself does not store anything on our website.
  • In the shop. If you mention allergies, skin sensitivities or medical conditions relevant to a service (for example a hot towel shave), we use that only to look after you safely.
  • Website visits. Our hosting provider automatically records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the website secure and working. We do not use advertising or tracking analytics.

3. Legal basis

We use your information because it is necessary to respond to your request and provide our services (contract), because we have a legitimate interest in running and securing our business and website, and — for optional cookies — because you have given consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Essential: we store your cookie choice in your browser’s local storage (key ab-consent) so we don’t ask again. This is needed for the website to work as you asked and is not used to track you.
  • Google Maps (optional): the map on our home and contact pages is provided by Google and may set cookies and collect usage data under Google’s Privacy Policy. It only loads if you click “Accept all” or “Show map”.
  • Fonts: our fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send the font files. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

You can change your choice at any time with the link at the bottom of every page, or by clearing your browser’s site data.

5. Links to other services

Our website links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. When you use those services, their own privacy policies apply.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the business (such as our website host and WhatsApp/Meta for messages you send us), or where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep messages and appointment details only as long as needed to arrange and provide your service and for normal business records, then delete them. Website server logs are kept by our host for a short period for security purposes.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, call us or speak to us in the shop. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

9. Children

We cut hair for boys of all ages. Appointment requests for under-16s should be made by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
